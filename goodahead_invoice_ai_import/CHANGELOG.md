18.0.4.2.0 — split into a core and an AI source, with the features of 19.0.4.2.0. The import
record, vendor matching, partner identifiers, vendor candidates and suggestions,
expense categories, the core profile, bank checks, UBL generation and decoding, the
failed-imports systray and the bill-form additions moved to the new add-on
`goodahead_invoice_import`; this add-on keeps the Claude reading (the extraction
call, the prompt and prompt profiles, the token-usage fields, the extraction-model
settings and PDF routing).

- **Upgrading from 18.0.3.34.0.** Run `-u goodahead_invoice_ai_import`. It installs
  `goodahead_invoice_import` first, whose pre-install hook renames
  `ai.invoice.import` to `goodahead.invoice.import` in place: same rows and ids,
  messages, followers, attachments, activities and job records re-pointed;
  settings, the mail alias and failed imports (still retryable) are kept. The
  upgrade refuses while import jobs are queued or running; installing the core on
  its own on such a database is refused too. Take a backup: there is no automatic
  downgrade.
- **New statuses** on import records: waiting, imported, duplicate, failed,
  possibly lost, closed without bill; *processing* is shown while the record's job
  runs. Old draft/processing records become waiting, done ones imported.
- **Duplicate PDFs are stopped, not billed twice.** A PDF whose vendor and invoice
  number already exist as a bill (same company, same kind, not cancelled) is
  flagged *duplicate* with a link to the existing bill; an Accountant chooses
  *Create bill anyway* (from the stored reading, no second Claude call) or *Close
  without bill*.
- Mail and jobs reach the AI through the core's source dispatch: PDFs come here,
  Finvoice files go to `goodahead_invoice_import_finvoice` when it is installed.
- Every 18.0 adaptation listed under 18.0.3.33.0 and 18.0.3.34.0 below is kept; the
  ones about vendors, products, bank accounts, the core profile and the generated
  UBL now live in `goodahead_invoice_import`.
- The migration folders are named after 18.0 versions, so a database coming from
  18.0.3.34.0 runs exactly the new step (`18.0.4.1.0`: existing upload and email
  records get `reader = 'ai'`).

18.0.3.34.0 — Odoo 18.0 builds from April 2026 are supported.

- Fixed: on Odoo 18.0 builds from April 2026 on, every import was refused with
  "AI Invoice Import cannot run on this Odoo core". Odoo backported 19.0's
  search-plan UBL import to 18.0 (odoo/odoo `86463ce1d`, "Fix import totals"). The
  module now recognises this build as `search_plan_18`, next to `legacy` (18.0 before
  April 2026) and `search_plan` (19.0).
- On that build vendors, vendor creation and bank accounts go through the same hooks
  as on 19.0. Products differ: 18.0 builds the product search plan in
  `account.edi.common._import_retrieve_products_search_plan` and has no vendor-scoped
  supplierinfo step. For a purchase with a known vendor the plan becomes this module's
  own supplierinfo step (that vendor's codes, same row), then barcode, so a vendor's
  article code is never matched against your internal reference. As on older 18.0
  builds, this applies to AI imports only; genuine supplier e-invoices keep Odoo's own
  product lookup.
- On that build a vendor IBAN that someone else (for example an employee) also holds
  is recorded on the vendor, as on 19.0, instead of being refused. The bill is never
  paid into the other person's account on any build.
- On that build Odoo can attach a substitute "Generated by Odoo" PDF to imported
  bills. 18.0 reads its switch (`account_edi_ubl_cii.disable_pdf_in_xml`) in
  `account.edi.common._generate_pdf_attachment`, not where 19.0 does, so the module
  did not see it. It now does, and turns the switch on at install and upgrade as on
  19.0. The vendor's own PDF stays the bill's main attachment either way.
- The Peppol endpoint written into the generated UBL follows core's per-scheme format
  rules (0007, 0208 and, new in 18.0, 0235 for the UAE: exactly 10 digits). A value
  that does not fit is left out rather than risking a failed import.
- Upgrading: update the module (`-u goodahead_invoice_ai_import`) after deploying, so
  the substitute-PDF switch is re-decided for the core you run. The core profile is
  re-detected at every server start.
- Tests: profile gates now accept several profiles (`skip_unless_profile(A, B)`), and
  the coverage check counts all three (search_plan 21, search_plan_18 22, legacy 12).
- Verified against odoo/odoo 18.0 `66d9d8da3` (legacy) and `465ae31c1`
  (search_plan_18): the full suite passes on both.

18.0.3.33.0 — backport to Odoo 18.0, with the features of 19.0.3.33.0. Differences:

- The generated UBL goes through 18.0's importer (`account.edi.common`): vendor
  creation is suppressed in `_import_partner`, vendor product codes are matched in
  `product.product._retrieve_product` (supplierinfo of that vendor, then barcode), and
  the vendor context is set in `_import_invoice_lines`. These act on AI imports only.
- As in 19.0, two bank guards also protect genuine UBL, Factur-X and Peppol purchase
  documents, which on 18.0 all go through `_import_partner_bank`: no IBAN is recorded
  on your own company or an internal user, and a received credit note's IBAN is
  recorded on the vendor instead of on your company partner.
- On an AI-imported bill, a stray copy of the vendor's IBAN on your own company
  partner no longer leaves the bill without an account to pay into (18.0 core neither
  uses nor creates the vendor's row in that case). An IBAN held by anybody else is
  still refused, as in core.
- A US routing number is stored in `aba_routing` (digits only) when `l10n_us` is
  installed, and otherwise stated on the bill once.
- A vendor with a portal login that Odoo's importer matches is no longer refused as
  "one of our own users"; only internal users are.
- **Create contact from this invoice** no longer fails with a validation error when
  `base_vat` refuses the tax id read off the document (for example a Canadian
  business number). The contact is created without it; the number waits in Vendor
  Data Suggestions and the chatter says why.
- The menus and the failed-imports bubble need `account.group_account_user` or
  `account.group_account_manager`. On 18.0 Community the first is the hidden "Show
  Full Accounting Features" group, so in practice accounting Administrators see them.
- The 19.0 history below applies, except the entries about the search-plan import
  pipeline and the substitute "Generated by Odoo" PDF, which 18.0 core does not have.

## The 19.0 history

The 18.0 releases above carry these features, with the differences they list.

19.0.4.2.0 — *Create bill anyway* on a duplicate PDF builds the bill from the
stored reading, without a second AI call. Needs `goodahead_invoice_import`
19.0.1.8.0.

19.0.4.1.0 — mail and jobs reach the AI through the core's source dispatch.
With the Finvoice add-on installed, Finvoice files go to it and PDFs come here.
A mail with no supported attachment now fails as "Format not supported"; other
attachments of a mail with PDFs are named in the chatter.

19.0.4.0.0 — the import machinery (import record, vendor matching, suggestions,
bank checks, bill decoding) moves to the new dependency
`goodahead_invoice_import`; this add-on keeps the AI reading, PDF mail intake,
token usage, prompt profiles and extraction settings. See the upgrade notes
above. Uninstalling this add-on now leaves the import history and vendor data in
place.

19.0.3.33.0 — several bills from one vendor imported at the same time no longer
make one of them fail. The failed-imports bubble and the import menus are shown
to Bookkeepers and Administrators only; access rights are unchanged.

19.0.3.32.0 — extraction prompts are a collection a company picks from. New
*Extraction Prompts* (Accounting ▸ Configuration ▸ AI Invoice Import) with two
built-in profiles, United States and European Union; duplicate one to make an
editable profile, and pick it in the new company setting *Extraction Prompt*.
Empty keeps the choice by company country. An inbound mail alias left behind by
an older version is kept as it is and only released from this module.

19.0.3.31.1 — the red banner keeps "(tax …)" on one line.

19.0.3.31.0 — the bill carries the PDF's lines only; any difference between the
bill and the invoice total is shown in a red banner for the reviewer.

19.0.3.30.0 — the imported bill's total equals the invoice's, tax included.

19.0.3.29.0 — an invoice that prints its tax only as one total is imported, with
a banner explaining how the tax was applied.

19.0.3.28.0 — the import no longer grants bill approval step 1; a person reviews
the bill against the PDF first.

19.0.3.27.0 — the import raises bill approval step 1 when the optional approval
module is installed (a soft dependency).

19.0.3.26.0 — every vendor-matching step, including the business id, refuses our
own companies.

19.0.3.25.0 — the unmatched-vendor explanation no longer shows on bills typed
by hand.

19.0.3.24.0 — the vendor line and the candidate card show draft bills as well as
posted ones; a bill with no candidate explains why; a new button creates the
vendor contact from the extracted data.

19.0.3.23.0 — a document's vendor is never one of our own companies, and a
received document's bank account is never added to our own partner. Another
company's partner is still allowed (the intercompany case).

19.0.3.22.0 — the country-aware phone key and its migration are withdrawn; phone
matching works as in 19.0.3.10.0.

19.0.3.21.0 — phone-key fixes (withdrawn in 19.0.3.22.0); a refusal by the
core-profile check is recorded on the import record.

19.0.3.20.0 — currency detection from the PDF: hidden annotations and form-field

older imports re-marked in both directions on re-import.

19.0.3.19.0 — currency detection also reads annotations and form fields, and a
short page that prints a currency counts as printed.

19.0.3.18.0 — whether an invoice prints a currency is now read from the PDF
itself, not from the AI's answer, so a domestic invoice without a currency is
marked correctly.

19.0.3.17.0 — fixes across vendor matching, the substitute-PDF setting and the
prompt; no new features.

19.0.3.16.0 — the email-domain exclusion list covers domains that identify

19.0.3.8.0 — product matching by vendor product code also works on the older

19.0.3.4.0 — the module detects which 19.0 import pipeline it runs on and warns
